Getting Started

The Free Time Tracking App Built for a One-Truck Lawn Care Business

Search "time tracking app" and almost everything that comes back is built for a business with an office manager, an HR department, and a dozen W-2 employees clocking in from a shared kiosk. If it's just you and a trailer, that software is the wrong tool — oversized, overpriced, and asking for information you don't have and don't need.

What "enterprise" time tracking gets wrong at this size

Team scheduling, shift swapping, payroll integrations, admin dashboards for managing other people's hours — none of it applies when there's one person on the crew: you. What you actually need is much smaller: know how long each job takes, and get that number without touching a keyboard mid-route.

What actually matters for a solo operator

  • Zero setup per visit — the timer should start because you arrived, not because you remembered to tap something
  • Per-property history — not just a total for the day, but minutes broken out by job site so you can see which accounts are worth keeping
  • No seat fees — pricing per employee makes no sense when there's exactly one
  • Works on the phone already in your pocket — not a separate hardware kiosk or a desktop dashboard you'll never open in the field

A free tier that's actually usable, not a trial

A lot of "free" business software is really a 14-day trial with a countdown. That doesn't work for a seasonal trade where you might want to test a tool on one job site through a slow month before trusting it with your whole route. A real free tier — one active geofence, no credit card, no expiration — lets you prove it works on your worst yard before you decide whether the rest of your accounts are worth upgrading for.

That's the entire design brief: geofenced, automatic, one-person-sized, and free enough to actually try.

No seats, no kiosk, no trial countdown — just install it and go.
