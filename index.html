Install MowClock

How to install a PWA

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the link above in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
  3. Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" -- done!

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link above in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right)
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
  4. Confirm -- MowClock appears on your home screen

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Is MowClock free?

MowClock is free for the first 45 days, with every feature unlocked and no credit card required — unlimited geofences, full historical reports, and CSV export. After the trial, Pro is $20/month or $200/year.

Do I need to install anything from an app store?

No. MowClock is a Progressive Web App — open the link on your phone and tap "Add to Home Screen." It installs like a native app in under a minute, no app store review or account required. An installable Android build is also available.

How does the automatic clock-in work?

Draw a geofence around each job site once. When your phone's GPS detects you've arrived, MowClock prompts a 1-tap clock-in, and clocks you out automatically when you leave. No manual timer to remember.

Does it work without cell signal?

Yes. MowClock is built offline-first for rural properties and dead zones — it keeps tracking locally and syncs the moment you're back in range.

Can it track more than lawn care?

Yes. Any route-based field business — landscaping, pressure washing, snow removal, trades — can use geofenced job sites and per-property time tracking the same way.
