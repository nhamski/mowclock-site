MowClock is a Progressive Web App. That means it installs directly from your browser -- no app store, no downloads, no waiting. Tap the button, add to your home screen, and you're clocked in.

Install MowClock

How to install a PWA

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the link above in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
  3. Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" -- done!

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link above in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right)
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
  4. Confirm -- MowClock appears on your home screen
